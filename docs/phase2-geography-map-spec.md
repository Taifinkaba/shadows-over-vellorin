# Phase 2 geography — map specification

This is a schematic design specification, not a surveyed map or player discovery. No scale, compass grid, precise distances, polygon boundaries or invented road geometry is supplied.

## Layers and legend

- Solid containment arrows: historically supported membership from `world-geography`.
- Blue containment arrows: APPROVED DESIGN, fictional relationships approved in Phase 2; no current observations implied.
- Dotted containment arrows: PROPOSED, including Vellorin → Aethoria.
- Solid travel lines: limited historical route reports; safety and present passability remain unknown.
- Dashed travel lines: PROPOSED corridors, excluded from operational navigation. Do not draw them as direct surveyed roads.
- Shaded belts: terrain/resource design; no exact borders, deposits or current supply inventories.
- Unplaced labels: established locations without sourced realm membership. Keep them unplaced rather than attaching them to the nearest drawn realm.

## Approved topology

Vellorin is the historical territorial umbrella for five political realms: Valedorn, Elyndor, Ironhollow Holds, Rivermere League and Frostmarch. Caelthorne is Valedorn’s capital. Existing Ironhollow Hold belongs to Ironhollow Holds; existing Rivermere belongs to Rivermere League. These design edges are stored once in `world-geography`, not in a competing map database. Aethoria remains the proposed continent. Northern Marches remains a sourced region of Vellorin with Brackenford and Briarstone Cottage under their existing parents; no realm assignment is inferred.

The five realm profiles own their climate, terrain, watershed, resources and frontier descriptions. Broad forest margins, uplands, passes, river reaches and frontier belts support border design; precise international lines, adjacency pairs and river confluences remain unsurveyed. Northern Frostmarch does not locate Frostspire Peaks. Forest/mountain/river resources imply neither ownership nor available stock.

## Route layer

Reuse `route-caelthorne-eastrun`, `route-caelthorne-rivermere`, `route-caelthorne-ironhollow-hold` and `route-eastrun-greyhaven-valley`. Add `route-northern-marches-frostmarch` and `route-elyndor-woodland-passage`. The last two join region scopes only, without invented gates. The Elyndor passage links the woodland realm to its Vellorin territorial scope; that is a corridor design, not a surveyed crossing to a particular neighboring realm. Local entry/exit and onward junctions remain unresolved. It cannot furnish an executable itinerary.

Existing four historical duration reports and seven archived proposed laden-wagon estimates are unchanged. Observed times are never multiplied or combined to derive mileage or cross-country links. Proposed reverse travel remains proposed.

## Cartographer and journey acceptance

Before operational use establish local gates, intermediate alignment, compatible terrain and mode, crossings, lawful access, seasonal availability, current weather/conditions, rest/water/feed and actual road authority/toll requirements. Record evidence in the existing route owner. Approval of a corridor or a realm does not satisfy these checks. Unsurveyed gaps must remain visible; do not imply a continuous road by crossing containment lines.

Preserve Greyhaven Mill/Valley, Blackthorn Watch/North Waystation, Frostmarch/Frostspire Peaks, Hollow Veil/Hollow of the First Hour and the distinct lodge identities. No new secret site, discovered entrance, hidden answer or NPC whereabouts belongs on this public design map.
