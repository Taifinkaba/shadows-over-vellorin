## Legal, adult-work and crime boundaries

lawful-design and regulated-design describe intended local cultural/legal treatment, not an enacted statute, verified license or immunity. Tolerated informal games depend on actual local custom; prohibited-design context covers fencing, forgery and other abuse without creating an operating illegal shop. PR3 will own formal jurisdiction/legal institutions. Pawnshops require documented lawful pledges; game stakes and losses are finite, voluntary and adult. No gambling debt is generated.

Amber Curtain and River Lantern House supply non-explicit adult entertainment designs. Every entertainment worker is an independent adult, may refuse or end engagements, controls boundaries/earnings/documents and can leave. Coercion, trafficking, violence, confinement and debt bondage are prohibited abuse, distinct from consensual adult work. Actual local permission, premises safety, paid staffing and supplies must be verified before use. No existing youth staff is assigned to these venues.

Every settlement has bounded risk/hook context: illicit goods, false measures/documents, restricted botanical or timber trade, cargo/mineral theft, coercive debt, unsafe labor, corruption and informal gambling may create stories after authorized play. No victim, guilty official, investigation, hideout, faction leadership, operating crime or event is established. criminal_faction_refs remains empty: authoritative eight-faction records belong to PR4, and no connection to protected mysteries is made.
