## Classifications and evidence limits

- **CONFIRMED CANON**: established current records or explicitly qualified historical outcomes, subject to governance precedence.
- **PROPOSED**: prospective architecture or design, including archived `[LOCKED DESIGN]` material that does not establish played events.
- **CONFLICT**: incompatible assertions or unsafe overlap requiring reconciliation.
- **UNKNOWN**: missing provenance, details or outcomes; absence from the index is not proof something never existed.
- **REQUIRES APPROVAL**: a player ruling or prospective design decision is needed before adoption; this audit grants none.

Explicit approved corrections precede played scenes, then latest approved entity facts/current checkpoint, historical evidence, and prospective design. GM truth never becomes character knowledge through retrieval. Expansion versions 1.0–2.2 have not been supplied as a complete versioned package in this request; the repository has an older World Bible v1.0 and reconciliations. A line-by-line compatibility verdict for unsupplied expansions, proposed officials, prison destinations, dungeon entries and main-quest answers is **UNKNOWN**. The named Continental Guild Concord is a proposal in this request, not an established indexed faction.
