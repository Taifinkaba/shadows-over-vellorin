It has no approved headquarters, branches, leaders, members, treasury, fees, commission schedule, geographic reach or operational authority. It does not establish Aethoria's borders or any continental government. Every division remains PROPOSED/REQUIRES APPROVAL:

## Funding, commission and completion

A future guaranteed reward needs an identifiable funder, cited commitment and sufficient committed integer-copper budget. Guild reputation or a posted number is insufficient. A proposed organization cannot issue an operational canonical contract or guarantee funds it has not established. Contract validation checks fields/references/arithmetic; human review must verify the source actually commits those funds, title, enforceability and conditions.

The existing10% registered-contract commission reference is preserved alongside the current policy-preservation instruction. It is not a new Concord fee, a retrospective deduction, a reason to infer historical gross/net amounts, or a charge on unrelated income. Confirm applicable terms before future play. Proposed dues, exams, commissions and renewal fees remain unapproved with no ledger posting. Academy finances, merchant goods and estate assets retain existing ownership boundaries.

During authorized play, accept a real contract only by player choice; record actual proof/completion separately; use the existing finance ledger for one supported payment with a stable event key. This implementation provides no payment/enrollment engine. It posts no commission, fees, reward, academy receipt, income or expense. Previously paid expenses and construction remain paid. [Party finances](../entities/mechanics/party-finances.md) and the existing ledger retain all accounting authority.

## Bounties, disputes and Chunk6 integration

A guild contract, arrest warrant, allegation, authorized apprehension, custody transfer, court review, conviction and sentence are different records. A bounty alone gives no unlimited police powers, immunity or automatic title to seized goods. Follow the [justice procedure](../entities/rules/justice-and-jurisdiction.md) and existing custody owner; no unapproved prison receives anyone. Disputes need an actual agreement or sourced lawful reviewer, not an invented guildmaster/court. No independent notice is canceled automatically and no historical confiscation is legalized retrospectively.

Dungeon contract vocabulary supports exploration, rescue, monster removal, artifact recovery, survey, escort, mapping, containment, salvage and research. Link a known physical site where actually sourced. A contract never proves a dungeon cleared, a monster defeated, artifact title acquired or treasure paid. No dungeon registry, encounters, sites, clearing or loot are implemented here; Chunk6 remains deferred.
