## Lawful, unrecognized, suspended, outlawed and criminal

The membership/status owner distinguishes five design classes. A lawful guild needs actual applicable recognition evidence before current recognized status is claimed. Unrecognized association does not mean criminal. Suspension requires an actual competent disciplinary decision. Outlawing requires a proved jurisdiction, lawful prohibition and reviewable decision; it is not a universal condition of a criminal-design label. A criminal faction’s unlawful-interest design does not convict members or prove any actual act.

Potential lawful consequences include bounded privilege/credential restrictions, a particular registration’s withdrawal or court-supervised activity limits only after competent decision, notice, defense and appeal. Other people’s rights/contracts remain intact. No ban, suspension, warrant, confiscation or judgment is issued here. The empty legal-decision template requires organization, jurisdiction, authority, legal basis, evidence, notice/defense, review and effective scope. PR 3 retains jurisdiction-sensitive investigation, business law, custody and court authority; neither a guild nor a rival’s claim supplies police powers.
