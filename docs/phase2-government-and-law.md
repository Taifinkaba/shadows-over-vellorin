This is retrieval and review guidance, not an operational authority list or case register. APPROVED DESIGN establishes fictional institutions and rules for future use, not appointments, exercised powers, currently staffed buildings, actual judgments or Theron’s knowledge. Historical fields retain their original classification/evidence; design does not fill gaps or overrule played canon. PROPOSED facility candidates and unverified local jurisdiction stay explicit.

## Criminal code and legal process

The 21 offense categories cover theft, burglary, assault, murder, fraud, forgery, bribery/corruption, smuggling, extortion, illegal detention, kidnapping, trafficking, property destruction, unlawful experiments/dangerous magic, mining/timber violations, contract fraud, cargo theft, proved coordinated crime and obstruction. Each distinguishes conduct/severity, evidence, five realm-specific investigation/local-trial/regional-trial/appeal references, proportionate sanctions and review limits. No offense entry is a charge or conviction.

Investigation needs credible particularized grounds; search/arrest needs competent authority and a bounded warrant or independently justified urgent necessity. Civil disputes and restitution differ from criminal guilt. Criminal findings require reliable corroborated proof, meaningful challenge and independent reasons; intimidation, confession or mental magic cannot replace them. Serious sanctions require enhanced review and explicit local authority. Exact penalty tariffs, maxima and local charters remain unknown and must be established before actual punishment; no mandatory fine, banishment, death sentence, forced labor, collective punishment, torture or confiscation is enacted by maintenance.

The process separates complaint/evidence, warrant/necessity, actual arrest, booking/care, independent review, release/conditions/remand, hearing/trial, supported verdict, separate lawful sentence, appeal/correction and eventual sourced disposition. Resolved historical judgments are unchanged. Court, military review, guild discipline, private restitution and citizen intervention retain different legal bases. Theron’s nonlethal preferences, rank, title, fame or property confer no blanket police authority.

The empty action template requires actual arrest/transfer identifiers, authorities, legal basis, subject, date/time/place, custody/receiving officers, evidence and property inventories, medical condition, transport requirements, departure/arrival/receipt, review and disposition evidence. It creates no record; actual supported events remain in the existing custody owner/chronicle. A building design, private agreement, proposed destination or unsigned warrant is no legal handover. Source truth, jurisdiction, chronology and competence still need human review.
