# Rivermere League

APPROVED DESIGN — fictional world geography, not observed Day181 conditions or player knowledge.

## Political identity

Federation of river merchant towns. Rivermere remains the existing river-trade settlement and is not renamed into the whole federation. Other member towns and charters are unspecified.

## Terrain, climate and resources

Temperate floodplains, riverbanks, marsh margins and cultivated terraces. Fish, reeds, farmland and river transport underpin the resource base. Connected navigable reaches are a design goal; depth, flood regime, towpaths, bridges and port availability remain unverified.

## Borders and frontier

River reaches and town jurisdictions are the border framework. Federation membership boundaries, upstream/downstream order and exact river crossings remain unsurveyed.

Containment belongs to `world-geography`. Weather, patrols, extraction rights, accessible supplies and occupancy remain UNKNOWN. No precise distances or coordinates are established.
