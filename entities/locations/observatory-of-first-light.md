# Observatory of First Light

Site of restored Chamber Seven and the [Time Chamber](../mechanics/time-chamber.md). The original facility used Stillwater and Quickening temporal configurations. Theron rescued Elyrian Voss alive here. The original [Vital Continuity](../mechanics/vital-continuity.md) archive was found beneath Chamber Seven. The private Day 326 feast for researchers, staff, companions and participating dragons established a milestone celebration tradition. Geographic placement and travel intervals remain unverified.
