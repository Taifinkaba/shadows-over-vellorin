# Mirror Repository

Westbridge repository with the Central Archive, Examination Chamber and Restricted Reflection Vault. The expedition examined HC-17 and LW-04 records here and ran two controlled paired resonance trials. Ilyr Varess is the former director. Distinct from the [Mirror Foundation](mirror-foundation.md).
