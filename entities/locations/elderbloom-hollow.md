# Elderbloom Hollow

Elderbloom Hollow, ancient woodland with springs, historical ruins and medicinal plants. Residence of [Sylmerath](../creatures/sylmerath.md), who offered to guide invited visitors. Exact location, road routes and distance remain unknown. No visit has occurred.
