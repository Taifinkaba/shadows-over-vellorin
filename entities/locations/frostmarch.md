# Frostmarch

APPROVED DESIGN — fictional world geography, not observed Day181 conditions or player knowledge.

## Political identity

Northern frontier territory governed by clan chiefs and seasonal assemblies. It is distinct from Frostspire Peaks and does not replace the Northern Marches.

## Terrain, climate and resources

Cold northern uplands and frontier valleys, with short growing seasons, winter snow and variable thaw. Pasture, hardy woodland, hides and preserved foods are finite resource possibilities, without current stock or harvest claims. Snowmelt streams and seasonally frozen waters require local verification.

## Borders and frontier

Open frontier and seasonal gathering routes define broad territorial limits. Relationship to the Northern Marches is a proposed travel connection, not containment or a surveyed shared border.

Containment belongs to `world-geography`. Weather, patrols, extraction rights, accessible supplies and occupancy remain UNKNOWN. No precise distances or coordinates are established.
