# Ashvale Grand Arena

Permanent arena at [Theron’s commercial estate](therons-commercial-estate.md), built and inspected for 1,200 spectators across 800 general, 300 reserved and 100 premium seats. Completed and paid cost 1,750 gp. Design allows a potential expansion to 1,800, which remains unbuilt and uncertified. Large-dragon exhibitions use the separate outdoor combat area and require each dragon’s consent. Session closed here at 7:00 PM on Year 851 Day 160.
