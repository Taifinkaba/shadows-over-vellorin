# Ashvale Grand Arena

Permanent arena at [Theron’s commercial estate](therons-commercial-estate.md), built and inspected for 1,200 spectators across 800 general, 300 reserved and 100 premium seats. Completed and paid cost 1,750 gp. Design allows a potential expansion to 1,800, which remains unbuilt and uncertified. Large-dragon exhibitions use the separate outdoor combat area and require each dragon’s consent. Session closed here at 7:00 PM on Year 851 Day 160.

## Grand Festival of the Year’s End

Year 851 Day 365 drew 64 entrants and sold 1,170 spectator tickets. Maeryn won the 100 gp prize, Theron placed second, and Aric and a veteran Guild competitor reached the semifinals. Lyanna and Kaela reached the quarterfinals. Tournament-safe equipment, inspected abilities, referees and medical review governed the nonlethal competition. See the [session rules and results](../../changes/chronicles/year852-day1-end-session.md#grand-festival-of-the-years-end). The session closed here at 12:00 AM on Year 852 Day 1.
