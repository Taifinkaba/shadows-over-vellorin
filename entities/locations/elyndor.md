# Elyndor

APPROVED DESIGN — fictional world geography, not observed Day181 conditions or player knowledge.

## Political identity

Woodland realm governed by the Circle of Rootwardens. This names a government design, without appointing members or assigning current actions.

## Terrain, climate and resources

Temperate woodland with shaded streams, wet hollows and forested ridges. Timber, herbs, honey and woodland products are finite resources; harvesting rights and actual yields require verification. Streams feed forest watersheds; no named river course is newly fixed.

## Borders and frontier

Forest edges and managed woodland approaches define a broad border landscape. Rights of passage and exact boundaries remain unsurveyed.

Containment belongs to `world-geography`. Weather, patrols, extraction rights, accessible supplies and occupancy remain UNKNOWN. No precise distances or coordinates are established.
