# Ironhollow Holds

APPROVED DESIGN — fictional world geography, not observed Day181 conditions or player knowledge.

## Political identity

Confederation of mountain clans. Ironhollow Hold is the existing settlement within the confederation, not a duplicate realm name or new fortress.

## Terrain, climate and resources

Cool mountain valleys, exposed ridges, snow-prone passes and rocky foothills. Ore, stone and limited mountain timber are resource design, not surveyed deposits or available inventory. Headwater streams descend through steep valleys; drainage connections require survey.

## Borders and frontier

Passes and watersheds form candidate border landmarks. Clan land claims and precise lines are unresolved; no new garrison or mine is operating by implication.
