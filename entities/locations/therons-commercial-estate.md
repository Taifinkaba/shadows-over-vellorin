# Theron’s Commercial Estate

Theron’s commercial property, separate from Aric’s ancestral Ashvale Estate. A paid 280 gp purchase, survey and registration from Merrick Vale added 20 acres to the starting 120. The resulting 140 acres include [Ashvale Grand Arena](ashvale-grand-arena.md) and the [Dragon Friendship Retreat](dragon-friendship-retreat.md).

Tomas manages operations. The monthly Ashvale Market Fair plan offers free market access and proposes 1 gp general and 3 gp reserved arena tickets. The first fair brought in 184 gp against 100 gp in paid expenses. The annual Grand Dragon Festival plan proposes 800 general seats at 5 gp, 300 reserved at 8 gp and 100 premium at 10 gp. The 7,400 gp ticket maximum assumes a sellout. Future receipts and dragon participation depend on actual events and consent.
