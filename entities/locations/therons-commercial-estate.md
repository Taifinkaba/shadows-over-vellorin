# Theron’s Commercial Estate

Theron’s commercial property, separate from Aric’s ancestral Ashvale Estate. A paid 280 gp purchase, survey and registration from Merrick Vale added 20 acres to the starting 120. The resulting 140 acres include [Ashvale Grand Arena](ashvale-grand-arena.md) and the [Dragon Friendship Retreat](dragon-friendship-retreat.md).

Tomas manages operations. The monthly Ashvale Market Fair plan offers free market access and proposes 1 gp general and 3 gp reserved arena tickets. The first fair brought in 184 gp against 100 gp in paid expenses. The annual Grand Dragon Festival plan proposes 800 general seats at 5 gp, 300 reserved at 8 gp and 100 premium at 10 gp. The 7,400 gp ticket maximum assumes a sellout. Future receipts and dragon participation depend on actual events and consent.

## Year-end festival and nursery

The Day 365 festival used the existing arena, outdoor exhibition area, lodge, market and estate land. Temporary pitches hosted 90 camping rentals and 30 merchant rentals. Tomas acquired Century-Bloom Emberpear planting stock and established a protected nursery on estate land with Ilyssara’s advice. Cultivation remains unproven and the immature plants have yielded no harvest or revenue.

## Year 853 session

Hosted the [Grand Festival of Dragons](../mechanics/grand-festival-of-dragons-year853.md), with 60 rented plots, temporary daycare and overflow camping. Reusable equipment remains estate property. Permanent entertainment and fair facilities and lodge/spa expansion are still proposals. DSI is separately located in Greyhaven Valley. Restored Chamber Seven is at the Observatory of First Light.
