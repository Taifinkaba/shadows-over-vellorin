# Ancient Dragon Instruction Grounds

The expedition explored a partly collapsed courtyard and chamber during the Hollow Veil instruction period. Draconic carvings referenced protection, inheritance and responsible acceptance of power. The inscribed passage remains sealed. The played discovery is separate from the unconfirmed historical temple. See the [Ancient Dragon Temple inquiry](../quests/ancient-dragon-temple.md).
