# Dragon Friendship Retreat

Private pavilion, strategy table, hearth and natural-form outdoor resting terraces built on existing [commercial estate](therons-commercial-estate.md) land for 340 gp, fully paid. Aurelith inspected and approved the finished arrangements. The retreat has no coercive wards or residence requirement.
