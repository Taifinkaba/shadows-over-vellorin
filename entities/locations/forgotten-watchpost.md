# Forgotten Watchpost

Abandoned watchtower north of [DSI](../factions/dragon-slayer-academy.md). Old protective runes and a stairway were found beneath it. The chamber remains unentered. Vaeltharion recognizes an older civilization’s symbols. The mechanism remains unidentified and no treasure or power was recovered. Open adventure lead.
