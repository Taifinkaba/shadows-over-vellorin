Original Guardian protections preserved. No Fifth Bell toll, Chronos Eye use/unlock or Dragon Slayer unlock.

Exact physical container/custodian unverified; do not fabricate it. [Accord](../items/first-hours-accord.md) is limited recognition/communication. Other Guardian channels remain damaged; remaining restoration, surviving Crown branches and eventual prisoner custody remain open.
