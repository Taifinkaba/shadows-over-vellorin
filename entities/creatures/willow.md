# Willow

Gray mare, riding horse personally owned by [Nessa](../characters/nessa.md). Passed basic soundness and riding checks at the Greyhaven horse market. Horse and tack cost 88 gp from the owner’s savings (95 gp before, 7 gp after). No proven warhorse training, combat bonuses or movement modifiers.
