# Vaeltharion

Ancient black-steel/silver-frost dragon with amber eyes; independent consenting guardian of Hollow Veil. Physically freed from all seven coercive chains; **zero attached chains, seven protective anchors retained**. Covenant based on consent. Sanctuary later functioned without continuous Theron mana under observed conditions; this does not establish universal independence under all stresses.

Dragon Master and cofounder of the [Dragon Slayer Academy/Initiative](../factions/dragon-slayer-academy.md) in Greyhaven Valley. Current exact physical whereabouts unverified; role does not imply continuous presence. Owns his scales; not a pet, guaranteed essence source, or automatically a sword donor. Prior Day46 junction testing remains historical, not the current freedom state. Existing illustration of seven attached chains depicts the older phase.
