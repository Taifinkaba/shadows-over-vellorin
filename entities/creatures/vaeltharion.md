# Vaeltharion

Ancient black-steel/silver-frost dragon, amber eyes, guardian of Hollow Veil. Original oath voluntary; later coercive fittings isolated. **Seven protective foundation chains physically attached**; Junction I tested and restored, six untested. Consents to joint research; owns scale stock. Wants to experience wind and forest outside sanctuary. Not a pet or automatically either sword donor.
