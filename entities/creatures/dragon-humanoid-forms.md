## Approval and autonomy

Established natural-form descriptions and relationships remain authoritative. Aurelith’s silver-haired, sapphire-eyed elven appearance and the corresponding transformation are player-reported historical continuity pending source reconciliation. Her detailed design and the other five visual designs await explicit approval. The Aethren and Vharos records preserve historical leads and proposals under research status.

Humanoid measurements describe depicted proportions. Apparent age describes the humanoid design, while the six dragons’ exact ages in years remain unknown. A depicted form grants no transformation access, resource costs, duration, combat benefits, resistance, equipment conversion or other ability. Metallic scales, spark effects and luminous coloring are illustration cues.

All dragons retain independent choices about travel, research and participation. Proposed designs establish no new encounters or obligations.
