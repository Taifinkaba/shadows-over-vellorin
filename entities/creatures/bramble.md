# Bramble

Bay gelding, riding horse personally owned by [Elias Renn](../characters/elias-renn.md). Passed basic soundness and riding checks at the Greyhaven horse market. Horse and tack cost 80 gp from the owner’s savings (108 gp before, 28 gp after). No proven warhorse training, combat bonuses or movement modifiers.
