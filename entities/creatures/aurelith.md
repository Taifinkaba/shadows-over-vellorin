# Aurelith

Independent ancient silver dragon, Keeper of Silverwake and friend of Theron. Theron visited, studied history and played strategy games. Aurelith voluntarily joined the first festival and private banquet, placed third in the exhibition, agreed to contribute preferences for a meeting place and inspected and approved the finished Dragon Friendship Retreat. Aurelith retains independent territory and choices.
