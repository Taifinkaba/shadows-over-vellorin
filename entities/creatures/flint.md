# Flint

Dark brown gelding, riding horse personally owned by [Gorren](../characters/gorren.md). Passed basic soundness and riding checks at the Greyhaven horse market. Horse and tack cost 90 gp from the owner’s savings (99 gp before, 9 gp after). No proven warhorse training, combat bonuses or movement modifiers.
