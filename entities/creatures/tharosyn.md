# Tharosyn

Independent ancient bronze dragon, Thunder Archivist of Stormvault and friend of Theron. Theron visited, studied history and played strategy games. Tharosyn voluntarily joined the first festival and private banquet, placed fourth in the exhibition and agreed to contribute preferences for a meeting place. Tharosyn retains independent territory and choices.
