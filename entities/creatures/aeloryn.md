# Aeloryn

Independent Guardian of the First Hour, conscious and willing to complete its original oath. Voluntarily accepted the Hour’s return; original restraints dissolved as the First Hour resumed safely. Granted [First Hour’s Accord](../items/first-hours-accord.md) voluntarily. Not Theron’s servant; original Guardian protections preserved. Other damaged channels remain open work.
