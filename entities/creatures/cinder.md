# Cinder

Chestnut mare, riding horse personally owned by [Kaela](../characters/kaela.md). Passed basic soundness and riding checks at the Greyhaven horse market. Horse and tack cost 85 gp from the owner’s savings (104 gp before, 19 gp after). No proven warhorse training, combat bonuses or movement modifiers.
