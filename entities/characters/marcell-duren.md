# Lord Marcell Duren

Northern Provisioning Consortium operations registrar. Documents show his office authorized late manifest amendments. During Day153 review observed by Theron/Nyx, his office acknowledged amendments under restricted Voss-authorized instruction. Cooperated and supplied a certified copy of restricted command correspondence; explicitly denied ordering bandit theft or guardian-ward sabotage. No guilty verdict, loot, confiscation or payment established.
