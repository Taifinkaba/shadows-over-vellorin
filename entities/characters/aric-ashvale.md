# Aric Ashvale

Theron’s father; former adventurer and A-rank swordsman. **At home at Ashvale Estate with Theron’s mother**, researching First Oath, Mirror Foundation and dragons. Personally commissioned black Runeblade; knows the smith’s identity and his own commission details. Knowledge of the white commission unverified.
