Theron’s uncle, rescued and receiving routine rehabilitation at Blackthorn. Physical improvement, but nightmares and memory gaps persist. Gentle consent-based mental evaluation/stabilization produced only limited or temporary improvement, not full restoration. Later declined another ritual; that choice stands.

**Uncertain memory fragments:** silver circular pattern, voices near a doorway, a man with a silver ring/dark stone. Not confirmed identifications or proof against any perpetrator.
