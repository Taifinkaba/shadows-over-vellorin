# Elian Voss

Original First Hand. His final memory established that he and Aeloryn suspended the First Hour to protect ancient Witnesses and intended its return when legitimate Witnesses reappeared. Incomplete succession left a protective Keeper-imprint whose interpretation became corrupted. The surviving imprint voluntarily surrendered its claim and dissolved peacefully on Day 472. Do not infer additional family relationships.
