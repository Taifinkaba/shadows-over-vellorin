# Commander Aldric Voss

Crown-linked antagonist associated with AV-07/CROWN-07 and forged paperwork. Not omniscient; agents and intelligence require actual channels.

## Through Day 153

His signature/mark appears on restricted N-17 routing authority. Duren’s office acknowledged manifest amendments under restricted Voss-authorized instruction. This does not prove Voss personally ordered theft or ward sabotage, and the new routing evidence must not be equated automatically with older AV-07/CROWN-07 markers.
