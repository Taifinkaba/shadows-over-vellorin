# Unfilled fixed-answer approval crosswalk

PROPOSED / REQUIRES APPROVAL. This public schema contains no unrevealed definitive answers, hidden actors, motives, locations, mechanisms or betrayal details, even in metadata. Resolved historical questions route thirteen existing objective sources rather than restating secret lore. Eighteen unresolved questions have unfilled answer fields. Unknown is not proof no answer exists. The blueprint is approval-ready structure, not an approved answer package or playable script.

For each unresolved question a future private design review must establish a definitive answer, cause, responsibility if supported, motive/method/timeline/location, evidence, discoverable opportunities, contradictions and consequences. Distinguish existing played facts from prospective design; preserve completed victories, identities, narrow probe knowledge and original safeguards. Obtain explicit bounded player design approval, then choose genuinely private storage/access controls before writing any unrevealed value. A gm-secret frontmatter flag in a public Git repository is not private storage. Public approval checklist updates cannot contain spoilers or disguised answer IDs. Publication approval and PR merge do not activate a story outcome.

Future legal/military reviews and victim dispositions are not prewritten verdicts. Their review slots concern established legal/provenance facts and bounded unknowns; no fixed-answer approval may automatically convict, release, transfer or decide an independent actor's future choice. Actual case outcomes still require supported play or an explicit ruling and their proper owners.

No new mastermind or organization is instantiated. The names suggested in prospective worldbuilding in the request confer no canon, accomplice role or hidden answer. Any such design must remain a separately approvable proposal; no new character/faction entity is created here. Current validator reports all missing approvals and unfilled route slots as nonfatal readiness blockers, not fabricated resolutions.
