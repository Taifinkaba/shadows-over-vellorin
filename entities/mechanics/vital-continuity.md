# Vital Continuity

The original archive beneath Chamber Seven referenced an Everlasting Seed. Theron and Maeryn found the Gardens of Ilyssara, and Ilyssara permitted examination of a mature fallen seed. Century-old botanical and healing records informed the selective restoration hypothesis.

Prototype I demonstrated limited restoration of selected tissue. Prototype II improved monitoring and selective treatment. Configurations that disrupted healthy tissue activity were rejected. Biological-age prevention, rejuvenation, lasting longevity enchantments, age-free chamber operation and safe years-long treatments for living people remain unproven. Research remains ongoing.
