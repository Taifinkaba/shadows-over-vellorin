# Ancient Dragon Combat Sheets — PROPOSED rules, not established canon

These three independent dragons have established identities, histories, autonomy, and festival placements, but their creature files provide **no approved numeric HP/AC/damage/saves/speeds or exhaustive power list**. All numbers and named combat maneuvers in this document are **new encounter-design proposals**, NOT retrospectively observed facts, innate powers proved in play, or upgrades to the existing canon. Do not treat these as official until explicit player approval. Do not overwrite creature entities from this proposed file. Keep hidden powers UNKNOWN instead of declaring the full historical capabilities of an ancient being.

## General proposed encounter procedure (5e-inspired, local rules only)

- Use grid feet, d20 attacks, d20 saving throws, hit points, AC, and 6-second rounds only **if approved**. Initiative: d20 + DEX modifier. Saving throw: d20 + listed modifier; meet or exceed DC to succeed.
- Action economy: one Action, one Move (up to speed), one Reaction between turns; each listed Legendary Action costs 1 point and can happen only at the end of another combatant's turn; max 3 points/round recovered at start of dragon turn, not during own turn. No more than one legendary action at a time. Legendary options are not additional spells or free mana.
- Breath Recharge (5–6): roll d6 at start of dragon turn after the initial use; 5 or 6 refreshes one use, otherwise remains spent. When breath is used, recharge availability ends. No automatic infinite repeated breath.
- Multiattack: one action; exactly the indicated attacks. Single Attack can be used instead. Reach measured from dragon's occupied space.
- Saves halve damage on success, rounded down. Resistances halve damage (rounded down), vulnerabilities double, immunities zero. Apply successful save before resistance. Specific conditions listed; otherwise no unlisted immunity. Dragon cannot spend legendary actions while incapacitated.
- Flight requires space; movement and landing matter. Wings/tail can endanger nearby allies; this is not waived in demonstrations. Nonlethal sparring: dragon and player can agree to end at a marked safe training touch or theoretical 0 HP; true hostile combat has lethal risk.
- Each sheet has a **proposed** stamina/breath model but no independent MP pool; dragon mana, casting, spell list and magic costs are UNKNOWN and cannot be invented during play. Existing character-specific rune/ward effects require established owners.
- No guaranteed win or idealized perfect intelligence. Use observed abilities only in Raze reconstructions; optional sheet abilities in hypothetical training must be labeled proposed and unknown in actual combat until witnessed.
- Proposed threat numbers are deliberately high relative to Level 5 Theron. Do not stage a real full-power duel without fair warning and player decision.
- ALL traits, saves, numbers and moves below are **design drafts**. Established facts in the lore paragraphs are the only canon statements.

## VAELTHARION — Black-Steel / Silver-Frost Ancient Dragon
**Canon identity**: Free independent ancient dragon, amber eyes, former Hollow Veil captive with zero coercive chains and seven protective anchors retained; DSI cofounder and friend. Won initial friendly dragon exhibition. Not a servant or guaranteed trainer.
**Proposed encounter profile**:
- Size Gargantuan; alignment and age-in-years UNKNOWN. Rank: ancient dragon class (NOT SS certification). Level/XP UNKNOWN.
- AC 23; HP 420/420; initiative +2; Speed 40 ft, Fly 80 ft, Burrow UNKNOWN, Swim UNKNOWN.
- STR 28 (+9), DEX 14 (+2), CON 26 (+8), INT 22 (+6), WIS 20 (+5), CHA 22 (+6).
- Saving throws: STR +16, DEX +9, CON +15, INT +13, WIS +12, CHA +13.
- Skills: Perception +19, Insight +12, History +13, Intimidation +13. Passive Perception 29.
- Senses: darkvision 120 ft; blindsight 30 ft; normal hearing, scent details UNKNOWN. Languages: spoken language with Theron; other languages UNKNOWN.
- Damage resistance: cold; immunity: none established (proposed sheet makes no blanket immunity). Condition immunity: frightened (proposed). Other resistances, weaknesses and magical defenses UNKNOWN.
- ACTION Multiattack: 1 Bite + 2 Claws.
- Bite: +16 to hit, reach 15 ft, 3d10+9 piercing.
- Claw: +16 to hit, reach 10 ft, 2d8+9 slashing.
- Tail: +16 to hit, reach 20 ft, 2d10+9 bludgeoning; target Huge or smaller DC 23 STR save or knocked prone.
- Silver-Frost Breath (Recharge 5–6): 90-ft cone, DC 23 CON save, 12d8 cold (half on save), failed save speed halved until end of next turn. PROPOSED elemental manifestation; black-steel/silver-frost coloring alone does not establish breath effects.
- ACTION Dreadful Presence (Recharge after short rest): creatures selected by dragon within 60 ft, DC 21 WIS save or frightened for 1 minute; save at each turn end. PROPOSED.
- REACTION Guarded Pivot (1/round): when enemy leaves reach, one Tail attack. PROPOSED.
- LEGENDARY (3 pts/round): Observe (1) Perception check; Tail (1) one tail attack; Wing Reposition (2) move up to half fly speed without opportunity attacks and creatures within 10 ft DC 23 DEX or 2d6 bludgeoning/prone. PROPOSED.
- Tactics: probe for retreat paths, preserve flank coverage; will call out reckless approaches in consensual sparring. Negotiation, withdrawal and protection of sanctuary remain legitimate decisions.
- Known limitations: No recorded numerical stats; cold resistance, breath and all moves provisional; original seven anchors not a combat resource or recharge pool.

## Combat log and validation contract

For EACH actual encounter record: date/time, location, participants and consent, starting HP/MP/charges, actual equipped AC/DEF, initiative rolls, round-by-round actions and dice, DC and save, target, damage and mitigation, movement, concentration, recharge die, legendary points, injuries, resource changes, witnesses, end condition, XP/skill evidence, owner of each fact. Provisional techniques should remain unused in canon until accepted. HP and wound recovery cannot be assumed overnight without applicable established rules. No numerical bonuses from simulated wins.
