# Time Chamber

## Year 853 Day 52

Occupied: 2× supervised, 5× up to eight internal hours, 10× up to one internal hour. Unmanned: 15× experimental and 20× repeated up to four internal hours. Above 20× unverified. Long-term target 525,600×. Physical acceleration, subjective time and aging remain distinct.

Restored Chamber Seven at the [Observatory of First Light](../locations/observatory-of-first-light.md).

| Acceleration | Played demonstration |
|---|---|
| 2× | Established supervised occupied operation |
| 5× | Supervised occupied, up to 8 internal hours |
| 10× | Limited occupied, up to 1 internal hour |
| 15× | Experimental unmanned |
| 20× | Repeated unmanned, up to 4 internal hours |
| Above 20× | Unverified |
| 525,600× | Research target of one internal year per external minute |

Initial 20× trials encountered thermal instability. External cooling and containment adjustments allowed longer unmanned runs. Occupied use at 20× has no established safety.

The chamber uses finite magic and requires emergency synchronization, safe exits, separate biological monitoring and living support. Physical fatigue, food, sleep, healing and biological aging still apply. Operating costs and long-term economics remain unverified. Acceleration provides no unlimited mana, automatic experience, mastery or combat-stat increases.
