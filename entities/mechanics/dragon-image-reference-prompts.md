# Dragon image-generation reference prompts

## Shared consistency rules

Use the exact height, weight and measured proportions as visual anchors. Keep apparent age, facial structure, ear shape, eye and hair colors, hairstyle, skin undertones, confirmed marks and small draconic traits consistent within each design. Preserve the signature clothing silhouette, palette and accessories. Keep unseen or unspecified marks flexible until the player approves a reference.

Show front, side and three-quarter full-body views, plus a face close-up and signature clothing and accessories. Keep the pose and lighting neutral enough to read the face, clothing and proportions. Use relevant approved artwork as a visual reference when available. Use [Vaeltharion’s existing natural-form illustration](../../artwork/dragons/README.md) in its historical context, preserving the natural-form depiction and separate humanoid design.

Written measurements guide proportion and leave variation between generations. Use approved visual references for follow-up generations. The supplied prompts are design references under review and do not establish an in-world transformation.
