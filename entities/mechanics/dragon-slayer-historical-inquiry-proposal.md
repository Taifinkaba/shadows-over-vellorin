# Historical Dragon Slayer Inquiry — hypotheses, not retroactive canon

## Repository audit
- Dragon Slayer ability is explicitly LOCKED by player choice; no accepted draconic essence. Training progress in Dragon Anatomy 5/5, Dragon Attack Recognition 5/5, Defensive Dragon Combat 5/5, Coordinated Anti-Dragon Defense 5/5 and Draconic Resonance 3/5 does not unlock it.
- The academy name does not certify that any student or Theron is a Dragon Slayer.
- Checked existing dragon creature entities, Theron skills and abilities, academy, handoff, archived mechanic ledger and open verifications. No verified named historical Dragon Slayer, living survivor, or recorded individual with abilities granted by multiple independent dragons appears in the examined sources.
- No evidence in inspected material establishes whether receiving multiple dragons' gifts is possible, safe, compatible, additive or permanent. Do not invent transfer mechanics or use a shed scale as unapproved power.
- Vaeltharion, Aurelith, Tharosyn are independent and retain autonomy. Conversations may elicit memories if they voluntarily share; statements must distinguish firsthand from tradition.

## Inquiry questions
1. Did past Dragon Slayers exist as an actual historical order, title, or draconic-bond tradition in the setting, as opposed to the present academy's name?
2. Are there living witnesses or dragons who personally encountered such practitioners?
3. Was any practitioner voluntarily aided by more than one dragon? Is that aid training, blessing, temporary channeling, essence acceptance, a relic or simply friendship?
4. What are safety, consent, energy, incompatibility, withdrawal and permanence conditions?
5. What is the location and custody of any surviving primary record?
6. What evidence would be necessary to unlock Theron's Dragon Slayer ability *if player actively chooses it*?

## Verification ladder
- Lead: rumor, motif, index heading, claim, or unverified speaker.
- Corroborated: two demonstrably independent witnesses or matched provenance records.
- Authenticated: valid primary record or witnessed mechanism, with chain of custody.
- Learned: Theron directly examines or is voluntarily told; GM-only background not automatically disclosed.
- Mechanically certified: player-approved requirements, explicit finite costs and successful documented trial.
- A title or name cannot be promoted from a lead to an existing NPC without played discovery; any new NPC developed in play needs location, motives, autonomy, and credible source.

## Proposed next playable scene
Theron asks Vaeltharion respectfully whether he personally remembers a person known as a Dragon Slayer and whether he ever witnessed more than one dragon voluntarily sharing distinct powers. Then independently ask Aurelith and Tharosyn for corroboration if they agree. Search pre-existing registers and historian/Waymark material, but do not treat title matches as proof. A historical Dragon Slayer can only be declared alive after an actual grounded investigation, not because this proposal seeks one.

## Interface to new encounter sheets
The proposed dragon stat blocks exist for adjudication only when approved. They are not observations of what the dragons have done and confer no information to Theron's tactical simulations until revealed in play. Actual battles use tracked finite actions and resources; no guaranteed wins or mental one-year-in-a-moment acceleration.
