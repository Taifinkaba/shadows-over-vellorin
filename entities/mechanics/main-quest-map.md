# Main quest owner routing

Retrieval only. The First Oath/Crown investigation owns the central conflict: original protective Witness/oath systems versus later coercive memory-taking, counterfeit authority and related surviving branches. The known First Hour cause is in its historical owner; this does not supply a universal origin or mastermind for every later conspiracy. Not all Wardens, Crown offices or guild members are guilty. CROWN-07, AV-07, V-9 and A-07 are distinct designations; a shared number is not an identity proof.

All public-reference quest owners are routed below, including completed rescues and research/last-verified records. Owner frontmatter and dated objective prose retain their exact limits. No status is copied into this directory. In particular first-hour-restoration is metadata active because wider Guardian work remains, although the First Hour objective succeeded; v9-memories metadata completed protects Lyanna's recovery/restoration, not every V-9 case. Older ACTIVE text under historical headings does not reopen completed objectives. The map is not a mutable master quest log, culprit list or completion percentage.

Relations to people, evidence, locations, factions, legal reviews, optional work and unresolved questions are retrieved from the linked owners and specialized crosswalks. Known locations do not certify a functioning road, present occupant, explored room or new clue. Secret quest owners/answers are excluded from this public routing; index access is never a player discovery. Read only relevant sources and later supersessions.
