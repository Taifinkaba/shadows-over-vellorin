# Original Wardens and successors

Original seven Wardens witnessed Vaeltharion’s voluntary oath with Eighth Witness Aelthir Ashvale. Later coercive alterations must not be attributed automatically to original signatories.

## Through Day 153

Later physical release retains the seven protective anchors and consent covenant. Removed coercive chains are not equivalent to destroyed protective anchors; original signatories are not automatically guilty of later abuses.
