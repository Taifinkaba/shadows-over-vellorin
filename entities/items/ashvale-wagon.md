# Ashvale Lodge supply wagon — Day 196

The player confirmed that the existing historical Ashvale supply wagon serves Ashvale Lodge as a vehicle distinct from the Sovereign Expedition Wagon and Vanguard. The refit used this existing stock. Its intermediate movements and any earlier identification with the expedition wagon remain unverified.

Refit complete and tested. Deep forest green paint with bronze trim and subtle emerald runes. See the [checkpoint](../../state/checkpoint.json) for the independent 80-point reservoir's current charge and the [ledger](../../state/finance-ledger.json) for the 105 gp refit payment (`y848-d196-supply-refit`).

- Self-propulsion, weight reduction and controlled difficult-terrain travel.
- Finite spatial cargo, structural, kinetic and flame protection, and finite automatic repair.
- Arcane locks, alarms, disguise and stationary camp shelter.
- Defensive nonlethal countermeasures. The refit has neither a dedicated Thunderous Ram nor a continuously electrified exterior.

The circuit has a dedicated emergency shutdown. Practical limits apply to travel, protection and storage. Exact activation costs, recharge rates, damage, DCs and capacity limits are unknown. The custom effects confer no standard 5e spells or skill advancement.
