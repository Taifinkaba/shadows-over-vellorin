# Century-Bloom Emberpears

Rare delicacy served at both the original Grand Dragon Festival and the Year 851 Day 365 year-end feast. The later supply cost 360 gp. Tomas separately acquired 120 gp in planting stock already included in event expenses. The protected nursery occupies existing commercial estate land. Stock remains immature, with future cultivation uncertain and no harvest or revenue.
