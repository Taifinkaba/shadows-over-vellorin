# Quest continuation, evidence and completion procedure

## Continuing play — retrieval, not automatic resolution

1. Read current checkpoint/status, source precedence and locks. Maintenance does not resume an ended session or adopt Day181.
2. Identify the requested active quest or scoped objective; resolve its original owner through main-quest-map.
3. Read relevant dated owner sections and later supersessions. Preserve victories without confusing objective completion with whole-quest completion.
4. Retrieve only relevant clue/custody/identity sources; separate authentication, physical observation, testimony, confession, surface probe, theory and allegation.
5. Establish which particular facts Theron and each companion actually discovered. Repository/index access, shared faction, similar title or a source link is not knowledge.
6. Select one meaningful next objective/decision with supported dependencies. Consult proposed plan readiness; unapproved secret answers/routes remain blockers, not invented leads. Ask for the bounded missing design or offer an existing known actionable lead without guaranteeing its answer.
7. Respect actual geography/access/travel, finite MP, equipment, staff/transport/budget, evidence title/safety and legal jurisdiction. A planning node does not create a road, secret site, free teleport or rest.
8. Present genuine compatible methods and consequences; allow delegation/postponement and independent NPC refusal. Nonlethal style remains available; do not make probing or executions mandatory.
9. Avoid repeated unchanged-evidence loops. Carry forward decisive discoveries, combine redundant scenes, distinguish optional lore and give bounded alternate routes once designed/approved. Do not guarantee rolls, cooperation or survival.
10. Update original quest/character/faction/custody/resource owners and actual chronicle/checkpoint only after played events or explicit rulings; post nothing from architecture. Do not award objects/money/ranks twice or move prisoners/actors.
11. Keep unrevealed GM answers in genuinely private approved storage. Public records contain only discoveries with provenance; never copy an entire mixed-knowledge file into the response.
12. Offer a genuine finite path toward a conclusion using approved essential-objective scope. For “how close” report completed protected objectives, remaining proposed essential questions and known blockers;14 scenes is a target, not a percentage or guaranteed remaining count. End-session produces checkpoint/handoff and stops.

## Completion and consequences

Before a proposed main-quest ending can be used, approve essential scope and safely stored answers, check source-compatible clue/scene routes, resolve or explicitly lawfully contain central risks, and record actual player decisions/results. Verify each essential objective from evidence, not a checklist auto-tick. Distinguish active, objective complete, main complete, failed, abandoned, deferred, partially resolved, consequences pending and unknown in outcome records without changing existing allowed frontmatter statuses. No status changes occur here.

A successful conclusion may be imperfect/costly: command responsibility supported, central V-9 harm addressed or lawfully disposed, Fifth Bell neutralized/contained, essential counterfeit authority addressed, surviving responsible opposition faced, Witness choice made and immediate consequences established. These are proposed criteria, not declarations any are satisfied now. Setbacks/failure/abandonment may leave consequences pending with honest source coverage; no forced punishment or survival guarantee. No capture of every minor actor, unanimous faction approval, all lore solved or every side quest is required.

Thane legal review and Sorn military review remain independent; confession/surrender is not conviction, villain defeat sentences no subordinate, property cannot be confiscated without lawful/approved basis. Keep V-9 cases, historical aggregates, Lyanna's vessels and unknown prisoner destinations distinct. Preserve protected originals/chain of custody; no free asset liquidation or retrospective transfer.

Faction/NPC responses require actual time advance/played scene, plausible trigger, resources and independent choice under Chunk8 procedures; no retaliation, villain movement, new conspiracy, clock tick, research or assignment result is generated. Finance, guild contracts, dungeon sections and legal outcomes stay with their owners. Remaining independent side quests and proceedings retain their own next steps after a main ending, not automatic completion or erasure.
