# Story Mode — Favored Protagonist

Theron is deliberately overpowered and favored; routine enemies should not become exhausting. Credible repeated investigation yields progress, finite missing requirements or an alternative. Permanent death remains possible with fair warning and agency. Never force quest progression or grind. Strict medieval high fantasy. Independent findable NPCs.
