# Chunk 2 — geography and travel maintenance design provenance

Source: player-supplied “CODEX CHUNK 2 OF 10 — Geography, Roads & Travel” request, supplied after Chunk1 PR#11 was merged. This is a maintenance design record, not a played-scene chronicle or approval of prospective geography. The request authorizes building indexed location/route records, reusable travel procedure, cross-system integration links, validation and a reviewable PR on `lore/world-geography-travel`. It expressly requires proposed geography to remain PROPOSED until approved and forbids campaign movement, resource changes or retrospective journeys.

The request names Aethoria, Ironhollow, Elyndor, Rivermere and Frostmarch among priorities but supplies no new map, borders, distances or intermediate settlements. Aethoria is a proposed continent framework; Elyndor/Frostmarch are proposed territory placeholders with unverified identity/type/position. No claim equates Frostmarch with Frostspire, Ironhollow shorthand with a different settlement, or Greyhaven Mill with Greyhaven Valley. Vellorin/Northern Marches/Blackthorn North Waystation names are supported by the existing Brackenford record. Established Ironhollow Hold and Rivermere names come from the archived map; its route estimates remain prospective under the later atlas.

The exact complete expansion1.0–2.2 package remains unsupplied. New connector proposals are review candidates, not navigable canon, surveys or approved historical roads. No new institutions, officers, prisoners, rewards, encounters, lore answers or current weather are established here. Existing checkpoint, ledger, assignments, inventory, quests and visibility remain unchanged. Day181 adoption remains outside this chunk.
