## READING KEY AND CANON POLICY
- **[CANON]** explicitly established during play or in World Bible; **[DESIGN]** fixed now for future sessions, not a claim about earlier play; **[SECRET]** GM-only motivations/contingencies, not character knowledge; **[UNKNOWN]** deliberately unassigned or not established. Never fill UNKNOWN by guessing during play.
- This is a **GM-facing document with spoilers**. In player-facing sessions, disclose only information Theron has actually learned.
- A **home/base** is where an NPC normally stays, not a guarantee of current location. A **routine** is a default when unoccupied; emergencies, travel, seasons, injury and obligations override it. Characters need sleep and travel time.
- **Reputation** uses five bands per settlement/faction: -2 hostile, -1 wary, 0 unknown/neutral, +1 trusted, +2 honored. **Initial bands are prospective design baselines, not retroactive rewards**. A witnessed action can change an individual opinion without moving an entire settlement's score. Do not give instant worldwide recognition to the Blackscale alias.
- **Knowledge separation:** Public reputation of *Blackscale* and known identity *Theron Ashvale* differ. Only named witnesses or verifiable reports can connect them. An NPC cannot react to secrets they have never heard.
- **Resource rule:** Gold, guards, beds, healing supplies, horses, legal authority, magic and information are finite. No unpriced instant services or impossible same-day long-distance visits.
- **Reaction rule:** Each NPC has values, wants, leverage, fears, and a threshold for cooperation. A good roll improves a plausible negotiation; it does not overwrite autonomy, ethics, jurisdiction or physical limits.

### Rumor transmission [DESIGN]
- Eyewitness rumor spreads inside a village within **hours**, to neighboring road stops in **1–3 days**, to distant cities via caravans/couriers over **actual travel time**. Magical messaging is rare and requires an established device, sender, recipient and cost.
- Each rumor ledger entry: **source | event date | first recipient | route | expected arrival | reliability | public alias/identity | corrections**.
- A rumor is not proof. Official records travel more slowly but have greater institutional weight; forged records can be challenged.
- Identity exposure is a separate clock: 0 no link, 1 suspicion, 2 local confirmation, 3 regional circulation, 4 guild recognition, 5 Crown public identification, 6 widely known. **Initial identity exposure not assigned globally; use local witness knowledge.**
- Settlement reputation changes by **one band** for a substantial witnessed deed; two bands only for extraordinary sustained action. NPCs may personally react sooner.
