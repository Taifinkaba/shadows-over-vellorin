## 2. RULES OF THE WORLD [LOCKED DESIGN]
- **Genre:** Strict medieval high fantasy: castles, swords, horses, dragons, enchanted crystals, alchemy, runes, guilds. No firearms, electronics, computers, modern machinery, science-fiction framing, modern medicine. Rare advanced arcane innovation chiefly Theron's domain.
- **Resolution:** Declare stakes, DC, modifiers, resources, and plausible consequences before dangerous rolls. Roll d20 + relevant modifier against DC; no retroactive target changes. Routine safe actions may succeed without rolling. A strong argument grants a fair hearing, not automatic victory. Consent and independent NPC agency matter.
- **Permanent death:** Death is permanent unless an already-established, paid-for and successful exception specifically exists. Healing does not reverse death automatically. No plot armor for Theron or allies.
- **Time:** Record date, local hour, duration, travel, watch shifts, meals, sleep, deadlines and weather. NPCs act while Theron rests. Research takes time; multiple complex projects cannot all finish in a single night.
- **Mana:** Deduct before/when spells resolve, account for failures, state regeneration/rest explicitly. No free teleportation or indefinite ward energy. Enchanted materials have limits and may be depleted.
- **Information:** Separate player knowledge, character knowledge, NPC belief, GM secret. Clues must be obtainable by multiple logical avenues; never gate the entire main quest behind one missed roll.
- **Progression:** Skills and spells have prerequisites, training, limits and failure modes. Chronos Eye and Dragon Slayer are not unlocked by proximity to a dragon. S+ power does not erase law, logistics, diplomacy or protective infrastructure.
- **Combat:** Distances, line of sight, armor, enemy morale, collateral damage, concentration, ammunition/consumables and rescue opportunities are tracked. Enemies may surrender, flee or negotiate; consequences persist.
