## 8. THE TWO SWORDS — ORIGIN DOSSIER
### Black Dragon-Scale Runeblade [CANON]
- Legendary shortsword, **1d8+3** base magical damage as established; runic amplification, draconic resonance, dormant powers. Gift from Aric Ashvale.
- Scales willingly gifted by **unnamed black-and-silver Frostspire dragon** about 26 years ago; dragon survived; Aric promised secrecy. Aric **commissioned** the sword. Its actual smith's name is not established.
- Frostspire dragon warned Seven Bindings were weakening and Ashvale blood mattered. Eryndra is a **hypothesis**, not fact.
### White recovered blade [CANON]
- Recovered in the first dungeon / Lyanna kidnapping investigation from the abductor's network. Frost/reflective signature; precise maker, original owner, donor and acquisition chain **unconfirmed**.
### Shared findings [CANON]
- Deliberately matched ancient craftsmanship; complementary inscription: **“WHEN WINTER GUARDS THE THRESHOLD...” / “...ITS REFLECTION SHALL REMEMBER THE WAY.”** Black has protective frost aspect; white reflective frost aspect. Their pairing does **not** establish the same donor dragon, same forging date, same forge or same smith. No newly unlocked combat power.
### Investigation roadmap [LOCKED DESIGN]
1. Inspect smith marks, tang/guard, metal composition and rune grammar separately.
2. Compare base draconic material signatures **under** enchantments, not just frost appearance.
3. Seek Aric's commission ledger and forge receipt; check Ironhand Compact or private smith.
4. Trace white blade through abductor's custody, stolen property records and A-07 case.
5. Cross-check Frostspire shrine inscriptions and consented dragon testimony.
6. Only then determine shared maker, whether one or two dragon donors, and original paired purpose.
**[GM SECRET — predetermined resolution for future investigation]:** The blades share a **single master design** and were completed by **the same smith**, but in **separate commissions**. The black sword used Aric's gifted Frostspire scales. The white sword used scales from a **different frost-line dragon**, acquired through an older legitimate trade, and later passed into conspiratorial custody. Their pairing was intended to guard and reflect an ancient threshold oath; it was not designed to enslave Vaeltharion. The exact smith's identity and the two dragon identities remain **unassigned secret slots** pending verification of any older established canon; they must be fixed before clues naming them are introduced, not improvised during revelation. **Do not reveal this paragraph as character knowledge.**
