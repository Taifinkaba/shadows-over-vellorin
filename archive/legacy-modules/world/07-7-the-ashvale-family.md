## 7. THE ASHVALE FAMILY [CANON + bounded design]
**Theron Ashvale:** protective, sarcastic, brilliant battlemage/runecrafter, Eighth Witness, Blackscale identity; seeks truth and safeguards those he loves. **Lyanna Ashvale:** younger sister, formerly abducted, rescued and safe at Blackthorn last confirmed; possesses relevant signature tied to A-07 case and Expedition 47-B. **Aric Ashvale:** father, A-rank swordsman, commissioned Theron's Dragon-Scale Runeblade using scales willingly gifted by an unnamed black-and-silver Frostspire dragon approximately 26 years before current events. Dragon warned of weakening Seven Bindings and unusual Ashvale blood; Aric promised not to reveal dragon dwelling. **Mother:** rare sacred healer; name, current whereabouts and full lineage not established here. **Caelan:** uncle, rescued and recovering from Witness-related injury, safe at Blackthorn last confirmed. **Aelthir Ashvale:** ancient Eighth Witness, opposed coercive alterations to original Guardian covenant.

**Family chronology [CANON]:**
1. In distant past, Aelthir serves as independent Eighth Witness to seven Wardens' voluntary pact with Vaeltharion.
2. Later generations alter covenant with coercive fittings; original legitimacy is disputed.
3. ~26 years ago Aric meets Frostspire dragon, receives scales willingly, later commissions black blade and gives it to Theron.
4. Lyanna becomes connected to restricted Expedition 47-B, Seventh Key, and an A-07 bank signature recorded 17 days before its discovery.
5. Lyanna is abducted; Theron investigates Ninth Vault and conspiracy, rescues Lyanna and Caelan.
6. Theron becomes active Eighth Witness, confronts the Hollow Veil and challenges Vaeltharion's captivity.
