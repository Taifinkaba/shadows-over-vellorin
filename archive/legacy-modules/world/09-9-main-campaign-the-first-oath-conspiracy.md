### Antagonist structure [GM SECRET — locked direction, not yet character knowledge]
- **Aldric Voss** is a senior human operator coordinating a faction that believes Crown control of guardian infrastructure is necessary for national security. He is responsible for authorizing forged paperwork and covert seizure efforts, but **is not omniscient** and does not personally control every agent.
- **Black Ledger Office** is a compartmentalized network; some members pursue power, others sincerely fear the Veil's collapse. Evidence can split them.
- **The artificial Witness** was designed to spoof oath recognition, not create authentic Eighth Witness blood or consent. It can imitate some signatures but has failure conditions discoverable from original covenant testimony.
- **V-9** is a stolen-memory archive/relay, not simply a monster or treasure chest; extracting evidence can endanger victims' memories if handled carelessly.
- **Fifth Bell** is a ritual authorization signal whose danger depends on forged credentials and active relay pathways. Its exact site and activation timetable must be discovered through clues; it cannot ring merely because the plot needs a climax.
- **The original seven Wardens** are not all interchangeable villains. Some signed voluntary protections; later officials or successors installed coercive fittings. Records distinguish responsibility by era.
