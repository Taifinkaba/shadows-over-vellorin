## 4. GOVERNMENT, LAW AND JUSTICE [LOCKED DESIGN]
**The Crown of Caelthorne:** Monarch (identity not yet established), Royal Council, circuit magistrates, local reeves and watch captains. A royal warrant can authorize searches or arrests but is contestable before a magistrate. Ancient Guardian covenants are distinct from ordinary Crown law; Crown officers cannot lawfully rewrite them merely by decree.

**Common law:** Murder, kidnapping, slavery/coercive magical bondage, theft, assault, fraudulent warrants, magical impersonation and tampering with public wards are crimes. Fines, restitution, imprisonment, loss of license, exile and—in rare grave cases—execution require lawful process. Self-defense and rescue necessity are recognized but investigated. Surrendered suspects retain legal protections; property seized as evidence is not automatic loot. Unauthorized use of mental magic on citizens may be treated as assault or unlawful compulsion.

**Jurisdiction:** Villages: reeve and watch; trade roads: patrol captain; guild halls: internal discipline but no immunity from criminal law; Crown districts: magistrates; ancient sanctuaries: covenant/customary authority subject to disputes. Vaeltharion is a consenting person, not Crown-owned property. Wolves remain animals with agency; Alpha is not owned.

**Bounties:** Issued in writing, target and conditions specified, reward paid only upon verified completion. Recoverable stolen goods generally return to owners. Salvage rights attach to abandoned/unclaimed property after reasonable inquiry, not to victims' property. Reimbursement requires receipts or witnesses. Bribery may work socially but creates legal risk.

**Trials:** Evidence, witnesses, warrants, and local political pressure matter. A successful intimidation roll cannot erase documentary evidence. Wealth and rank affect access, not automatic immunity.
