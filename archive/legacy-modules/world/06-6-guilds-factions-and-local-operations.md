## 6. GUILDS, FACTIONS AND LOCAL OPERATIONS [LOCKED DESIGN unless noted]
| Organization | Headquarters / branches | Function | Internal behavior and constraints |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Wayfarers' Guild** | Caelthorne HQ; Eastrun, Briarfall, Rivermere | Contracts, escorts, expeditions, bounties | Requires reports, takes 10% commission on registered contracts; investigates missing teams |
| **Ironhand Smiths' Compact** | Ironhollow; Caelthorne chapter | Weapons, armor, metallurgy | Protects craft secrets; verifies unusual materials; may refuse unlawful commissions |
| **Circle of Seven Lamps** | Caelthorne; Blackthorn healers' affiliate | Healing, herbcraft, hospice | Patient confidentiality; charges by means; condemns coercive experimentation |
| **Scribes' Collegium** | Caelthorne; vault archive offices | Records, languages, magical inscriptions | Restricted collections need permission; rival archivists compete for discoveries |
| **River Charter League** | Rivermere; Southbank Landing | Barges, toll negotiations, insurance | Enforces contracts and embargoes; vulnerable to smuggling |
| **Greenward Covenant** | Elderwood/Silverbrook | Rangers, sacred groves, spirit diplomacy | Defends forest customs; distrusts Crown clear-cutting |
| **Stonewatch Wardens** | Blackthorn, northern roads | Patrols, rescue, guardian alerts | Responds to credible threats; chain of command and finite manpower |
| **Black Ledger Office** [CANON] | Hidden cells, Crown-linked paperwork | Clandestine records and manipulation | Uses cutouts, forged authority, stolen memories; true leadership unresolved to player |
| **Crown command network / CROWN-07** [CANON] | Royal apparatus, vault sites | Contested control of guardian systems | Not all Crown officers are conspirators; provenance must be proven |
| **Seven Wardens' legacy** [CANON] | Ancient covenant sites | Original Hollow Veil protection | Original voluntary oath later corrupted by unauthorized coercive additions |

**Village-by-village operations:** Dunmere favors Wayfarers and food merchants; Hearthford supports caravans and rumor brokers; Silverbrook answers first to Greenward; Red Quarry is influenced by Ironhand; Southbank Landing by River Charter; Whiteglass Outpost by rescue wardens and mountain guides. Guilds have dues, schedules, political tensions, finite stock and reputations. No guild can instantly solve every problem.
