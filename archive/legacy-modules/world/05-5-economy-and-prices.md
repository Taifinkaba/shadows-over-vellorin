## 5. ECONOMY AND PRICES [LOCKED DESIGN]
**Currency:** 10 copper (cp) = 1 silver (sp); 10 silver = 1 gold (gp). Thus 1 gp = 100 cp. Prices are reference bands, not universal fixed prices; scarcity, danger, season, taxes and negotiation affect them. No unearned gold appears.

| Item/service | Baseline price |
|---|---:|
| Bread, vegetables, simple daily food | 2–5 cp |
| Hearty tavern meal | 1–3 sp |
| Common inn bed, night | 3–7 sp |
| Private inn room, night | 1–2 gp |
| Horse stable/feed, night | 1–3 sp per horse |
| Basic travel rations, day | 2–5 sp |
| Ordinary labor, day | 2–5 sp |
| Skilled craft labor, day | 1–3 gp |
| Local guide, day | 5 sp–2 gp |
| Common healing herbs/bandages | 2–8 sp |
| Basic sword | 8–20 gp |
| Riding horse | 50–100 gp |
| Wagon | 35–80 gp |
| River ferry, passenger | 1–5 sp |
| Bridge toll, wagon | 2–8 sp |
| Minor registered bounty | 5–20 gp |
| Dangerous outlaw bounty | 25–100 gp |
| Enchanted item | Appraised individually; no universal price |

**Markets:** Caelthorne sells breadth at higher rent/taxes; Eastrun sells food cheaply after harvest; Ironhollow metal cheaper locally, imported food dearer; Frostspire supplies costly in winter; Rivermere prices depend on river transport. A village cannot instantly buy hundreds of gp of dragon materials; wealthy buyers attract scrutiny. Dragon scales are Vaeltharion's property and subject to his consent. Buying land, shops or guild shares requires documentation, tax, maintenance and time.

**Party accounting:** Separate personal coin, reserve, borrowed funds, custody/evidence funds and inventory value. No splitting rewards twice. NPCs independently earn/spend money, but major changes are reported in campaign ledger.
