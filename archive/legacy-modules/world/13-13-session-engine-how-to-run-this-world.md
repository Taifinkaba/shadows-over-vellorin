## 13. SESSION ENGINE / HOW TO RUN THIS WORLD [LOCKED DESIGN]
At the start of every session:
1. Print checkpoint (date/time/location, HP/MP/Defense, money by owner, inventory, party, wolves, wagon, active deadlines).
2. Identify which canon facts matter and what each character actually knows.
3. Determine NPC plans and faction clock triggers for the elapsed period.
4. Present environment, rumors, people, costs and at least one noncombat option.
5. Before risky action state objective, time, mana/material cost, DC and consequences.
6. Roll/resolve, update persistent ledger, and honor independent NPC choices.
7. End with exact checkpoint, progress, unresolved clues, choices. Do not fabricate treasure or spend other characters' money without consent.

**DM ledger fields:** `date/time | location | character HP/MP/status | party positions | inventory (owner, quantity, source) | personal/shared/custody gold | active quests | evidence and provenance | known NPC beliefs | secret NPC goals | faction clocks | contracts/deadlines | settlement stock/prices | world changes | next consequence`.

**Continuity protocol:** Before revealing a mystery answer, search this bible's CANON and GM SECRET sections. If a secret is marked unassigned, decide and record it **before** dropping the first specific clue; if it contradicts a recovered earlier established fact, earlier canon wins. Do not present DM SECRET text to Theron during normal play. If user requests a spoiler-free player handout, generate one omitting secrets.
