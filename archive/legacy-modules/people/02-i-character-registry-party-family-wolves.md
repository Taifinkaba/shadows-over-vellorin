## I. CHARACTER REGISTRY — PARTY, FAMILY, WOLVES
Each entry defines **base; role; relationships; goal; assets/constraints; ordinary routine; response to Blackscale; independent trigger**.

### Theron Ashvale / Blackscale [CANON]
**Base:** mobile expedition wagon; family ties to Ashvale Estate. **Role:** S+ battlemage, Eighth Witness, advanced runecrafter. **Relations:** Lyanna (sister), Aric (father), Caelan (uncle); Garrick, Nyx, Lyra (companions); Shade (bonded); Vaeltharion (friend). **Goal:** protect family, expose counterfeit Witness conspiracy, free Vaeltharion safely, investigate twin blades and dragons. **Assets:** as fixed World Bible checkpoint, not duplicated here. **Routine:** determined by player. **Reputation:** Blackscale identity is not assumed publicly equivalent to Theron. **Autonomy:** player-controlled; never narrate major choices for him.

### Garrick [CANON core; DESIGN routine]
**Base:** party wagon/temporary camps; **role:** frontline fighter, watch leader, wagon logistics. **Relationships:** respects Theron, friendly rivalry with Nyx, trusts Lyra's medical judgment. **Goal:** keep party alive, earn honest pay, keep horses and wagon functional. **Assets:** own arms, field kit, personal 95 gp 8 sp; limited stamina and supply. **Routine:** dawn weapons inspection, daytime scouting/escort or training, dusk wagon and horse checks, assigned night watch. **Blackscale response:** loyal but challenges reckless plans; will not quietly risk civilians or party funds. **Offscreen trigger:** independently buys routine supplies only from his own money; seeks work when idle; warns if a route is unsafe.

### Nyx [CANON core; DESIGN routine]
**Base:** party camp; **role:** scout, infiltrator, negotiator. **Relationships:** Ember (bonded wolf), Garrick (banter), Lyra (mutual care), Theron (earned trust). **Goal:** freedom, good contracts, protect Ember and allies, maintain useful contacts. **Assets:** scouting kit, personal 94 gp 8 sp; information network is local, not omniscient. **Routine:** dawn perimeter survey, daytime scouting/market bargaining, evening rumor collection, rotating watch. **Blackscale response:** teasing and loyal, but refuses plans endangering Ember needlessly. **Offscreen trigger:** accepts small local jobs, may withhold a dangerous rumor until corroborated; records her expenditures.

### Lyra [CANON core; DESIGN routine]
**Base:** party camp or temporary healer's shelter; **role:** healer and conscience. **Relationships:** Garrick and Nyx (companions), Theron (trust), local patients (independent obligations). **Goal:** prevent avoidable suffering, develop healing craft, keep companions safe. **Assets:** healing supplies, personal 92 gp 8 sp; limited time and ingredients. **Routine:** morning health checks, daytime treatment/foraging or travel, dusk inventory and recovery. **Blackscale response:** supportive of protecting people; objects to coerced mind magic, neglect of patients and dangerous tests. **Offscreen trigger:** volunteers at an infirmary or requests supplies if casualties rise; she may decline paid work to help the poor.

### Lyanna Ashvale [CANON core; DESIGN routine]

### Vaeltharion [CANON core; DESIGN routine]
**Base:** Mount Vaelspire Hollow Veil, physically tethered to seven intact foundation chains. **Role:** ancient dragon, Guardian; free-willed, not Crown property. **Relationships:** Blackscale (friend), historical Aelthir, seven Wardens' legacy. **Goal:** preserve Veil and gain genuine freedom of movement; visit stars, mountain forests and eventually western sea. **Assets:** immense but finite draconic magic, scales he owns, guardian knowledge; not a free mana source or instant teleport. **Routine:** watch Veil, conserve power, inspect disturbances, converse with visitors; cannot leave physical range yet. **Blackscale response:** warm, dry humor; consents to tests individually and can refuse dangerous changes. **Offscreen trigger:** alerts party to real anchor fluctuations; does not detach himself without safe proof. **Status:** Junction I tested then restored; six untested; small spatial activation only.
