## III. SETTLEMENT OPERATING RULES
Each entry lists **size/governance, economy, laws, guild footprint, routine, reputation response, crisis behavior, available services and limitations**. All new local leaders and hours below are [DESIGN] unless already named in World Bible.

### CAELTHORNE — Crown City
**Scale:** capital; population deliberately unassigned pending cartographic survey. **Rule:** Crown magistrates, watch, noble council. **Trade:** records, luxury crafts, metalwork, enchanted commissions, grain imports. **Guilds:** Wayfarers HQ, Scribes' Collegium, Circle of Seven Lamps, Ironhand chapter. **Hours:** markets dawn–dusk; archive public desk late morning–afternoon; guild halls daytime; night gates guarded. **Law:** warrants reviewable, contraband and magical impersonation prosecuted; unauthorized mind magic investigated. **Blackscale reputation:** 0 public; some officials may know Theron name or suspicious warrant, but identity linkage requires evidence. **Response:** +1 opens appointments and sponsors; -1 increases inspections and delays; -2 triggers lawful pursuit only on actual charges. **Crisis:** rationing and curfews require public decrees; guards finite. **Services:** specialist appraisal, courts, libraries, wealthy buyers, high prices. **Constraint:** restricted archives require authorization; noble influence can obstruct but not rewrite facts.

### EASTRUN — Farming and trade region
**Scale:** crossroads and farms. **Rule:** local reeve, merchants, circuit magistrate. **Trade:** grain, orchards, livestock, wagons. **Guilds:** Wayfarers branch, grain merchants. **Hours:** farm work dawn–late afternoon; market busiest after harvest. **Law:** theft of stores and livestock treated seriously; Crown requisitions need papers. **Reputation:** 0; helping farms earns +1 locally; coercion harms trust. **Crisis:** poor harvest raises food prices and contract pay; merchants reroute. **Services:** rations, stables, basic healing; limited enchanted stock.

### DUNMERE — Eastrun grain village [DESIGN]
**Population:** ~650. **Leader:** Reeve Mara Fenwick. **Economy:** grain, milling, cider, seasonal labor; farm prices favorable after harvest. **Guilds:** millers' cooperative; visiting Wayfarers. **Hours:** mill before sunrise to late afternoon, reeve petitions at midday, tavern dusk. **Law:** public grain scales, theft restitution, written requisition checks; no unauthorized armed levy. **Blackscale reaction:** 0 initially; rescuing workers or paying fairly gains local trust; showing Crown coercion papers without verification alarms villagers. **Crisis:** harvest loss triggers ration council and guarded granaries. **Services:** bread, fodder, cart repairs, no high-end forge. **Persistent NPC:** Mara Fenwick — reeve; home near meeting hall; goals keep granaries safe and avoid Crown seizure; assets village watch (6 part-time), ledger, modest tax chest; routine dawn grain review, midday petitions, dusk rounds; Blackscale cooperates if evidence and fair terms, refuses blanket confiscations.

### BRIARFALL CROSSING — Bridge town
**Scale:** toll and transit hub. **Rule:** bridge reeve and toll guards. **Trade:** bridge tolls, inns, horse supplies, river crossings. **Guilds:** Wayfarers office, caravan factors. **Hours:** bridge dawn–night with limited late crossings; toll desk staffed in shifts. **Law:** posted tolls, cargo inspections, ferry safety; toll fraud investigated. **Reputation:** 0; escort success improves merchant trust, bypassing tolls hurts. **Crisis:** flood or sabotage shuts crossing and raises detour costs. **Services:** lodging, wagons, rumors, contract boards; crowded in storms.

### HEARTHFORD — Caravan hamlet [DESIGN]
**Population:** ~280. **Leader:** informal innkeepers' council. **Economy:** stable fees, wagon repair, cooked meals. **Guilds:** visiting Wayfarers; no full guild hall. **Hours:** inn open late, stables staffed dawn and dusk, smith works daylight. **Law:** property damage restitution; stable quarantine for sick horses. **Reputation:** 0; helping stranded caravans gains goodwill, bullying staff spreads rumors along road. **Crisis:** storms fill rooms, prices rise within posted bounds, overflow shelter possible. **Services:** Copper Lantern inn, horses, repairs. **Persistent NPC:** Oren Pell — stablemaster; home beside Copper Lantern yard; goal maintain healthy horses and repay family debt; assets 12 stalls, 2 helpers, feed inventory; routine pre-dawn feed, daytime shoeing coordination, dusk checks; Blackscale welcomed if Bracken/Morrow cared for, refuses unsafe overloading.

### BLACKTHORN WATCH — Fortified refuge
**Scale:** garrison and healing refuge. **Rule:** watch captain under emergency authority, circuit oversight. **Trade:** patrol provisions, medical supplies, escort contracts. **Guilds:** Stonewatch Wardens, Seven Lamps affiliate. **Hours:** watch 24/7 in shifts; infirmary triage continuous; visitors recorded. **Law:** evidence and witness protection, weapons peace-bonded in infirmary, custody requires record. **Reputation:** +1 among specific rescue witnesses; broader garrison 0 until briefed. **Crisis:** injured civilians take priority over bounty claims, patrols reduced when beds full. **Services:** basic medical care, safe rooms, escorts, official testimony. **Limit:** cannot indefinitely shelter everyone; Caelan and Lyanna have agency.

### ASHVALE ESTATE — Ancestral holding
**Scale:** estate and dependent households. **Rule:** steward under Crown law, disputed warrant access. **Trade:** land produce, family stores, historical records. **Guilds:** none permanent; Scribes or Ironhand visitors by contract. **Hours:** estate work dawn–dusk; family archives by permission. **Law:** inheritance and search warrants require documentation; household staff retain rights. **Reputation:** Theron known as family, but individual servants' loyalties vary. **Crisis:** legal seizure or forged warrants can freeze accounts and lock records, not retroactively erase evidence. **Services:** family records and training grounds if accessible. **Persistent NPC:** Estate steward **unnamed [UNKNOWN]**; role, motivations and routines must be fixed before first encounter, not assumed corrupt.

### NORTHERN VAULT DISTRICT / NINTH VAULT
**Scale:** restricted ancient complex, not ordinary market settlement. **Rule:** contested Crown access vs original Guardian jurisdiction. **Trade:** none legitimate inside sealed areas. **Guilds:** Scribes' Collegium permitted expeditions; Crown surveyors; Black Ledger covert infiltration. **Hours:** patrol shifts and access permits, not free public visiting. **Law:** trespass and ward tampering investigated; legitimate evidence preservation supersedes looting. **Reputation:** researchers value proof; guards obey orders unless persuaded through authority. **Crisis:** lockdowns restrict exits and increase danger. **Services:** records, ruins, dangerous magical infrastructure, not vendors.

### MIRROR FOUNDATION
**Scale:** concealed oath infrastructure. **Rule:** ancient covenant procedures; contested Crown intrusion. **Trade/guilds:** none. **Hours:** guardian mechanisms persist continuously. **Law:** consent/authenticated Witness rights; forged commands unlawful under original covenant. **Reputation:** magical systems verify signatures, not fame. **Crisis:** tampering can affect guardian channels and stolen memories. **Services:** original inscriptions and testimony, only after safe access.

### MOUNT VAELSPIRE / HOLLOW VEIL
**Scale:** dragon sanctuary, mountain approaches and lower camp. **Rule:** original covenant and Vaeltharion's consent. **Trade:** no ordinary marketplace; occasional mountain guides on approaches. **Guilds:** no automatic Crown authority. **Hours:** protective anchors continuous; access by invitation and safe passage. **Law:** no taking shed scales without dragon's permission, no altering chains without verified safeguards. **Reputation:** Vaeltharion +2 personal trust; outsiders may know nothing. **Crisis:** genuine anchor instability triggers monitoring and conservative defense, not scripted apocalypse. **Services:** dragon lore, scales by consent, research site. **Limit:** Vaeltharion remains tethered at checkpoint; seven anchors intact.

### IRONHOLLOW HOLD — Smithing fortress
**Scale:** mining/smithing stronghold. **Rule:** castellan, Ironhand Compact. **Trade:** ore, metal, armor, forge labor. **Guilds:** Ironhand headquarters, Wayfarer contracts. **Hours:** mines and forges in shifts; licensing desk daylight. **Law:** forge stamps and material provenance, dangerous magical smelting permits, worker safety. **Reputation:** 0; skilled designs earn interest, unproven dragon-scale claims invite appraisal. **Crisis:** mine collapse redirects workers and increases iron price. **Services:** elite metalwork and smith archives; custom work takes days/weeks.
