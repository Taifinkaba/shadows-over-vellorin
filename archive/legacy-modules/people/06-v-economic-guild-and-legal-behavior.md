## V. ECONOMIC, GUILD AND LEGAL BEHAVIOR
- **Currency:** 10 cp = 1 sp; 10 sp = 1 gp. Use World Bible prices; do not create unlimited discounts or infinite buyers.
- **Daily budgets:** 2–5 sp ordinary labor; 1–3 gp skilled craft; a small village tax chest cannot casually pay a 100 gp bounty. Large commissions require deposits, days/weeks of work and proven material ownership.
- **Inventory restock:** village food daily/weekly by harvest; smith components weekly with shipments; specialist magic months/commission-only. No universal shop inventory.
- **Guild behavior:** Wayfarers takes 10% on registered contracts; Ironhand checks provenance and protects trade secrets; Seven Lamps triages by need; Scribes controls restricted records; River Charter tracks manifests; Greenward enforces grove custom; Stonewatch prioritizes rescue and patrol.
- **Legal escalation:** complaint → witness/evidence collection → local inquiry → warrant or hearing → magistrate decision → enforcement/appeal. Emergency defense may bypass some steps but remains reviewable. Noble, guild, Crown and sanctuary authorities can dispute jurisdiction; none automatically wins.
- **Property:** Vaeltharion's scales remain his unless gifted/consented for a defined use. Captured suspects' goods and stolen goods are not free loot. Personal coin is not shared by default.
- **Service capacity:** an infirmary can run out of beds, an inn of rooms, a guild of available escorts, a guard post of patrols, and a smith of coal/ore. Track depletion and resupply.
