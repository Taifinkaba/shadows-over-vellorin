## VI. OFFSCREEN NPC ACTIONS AND SETTLEMENT TURNS
**Turn cadence:** 1 local turn per **meaningful day** in a settled region; 1 regional turn per **3–7 days** or major event. Do not advance clocks simply because a scene takes several minutes. Record outcomes and communication delay.

### At each turn
1. Check each active NPC's location, health, obligations, funds, supplies, relationships and last contact.
2. Choose one feasible **goal-driven action** and one possible reaction to world events; do not make every NPC act on every turn.
3. Apply travel time, money, staffing, permits, fatigue and consequences.
4. Determine whether a relevant rumor or message has actually arrived.
5. Advance faction clocks only when a causal trigger exists, and foreshadow important shifts.
6. Record exact changes; never retroactively insert a surprise action without a trail.
