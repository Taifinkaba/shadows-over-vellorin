## 4.3 Travel and logistics procedure
1. State origin, destination, route, mode (walking, horse, wagon, river, magical) and departure.
2. Check known route graph, terrain, season, weather and border/toll constraints.
3. Declare baseline time and provisions **before** departure; avoid invented exact mileage.
4. Advance hours/days with watch shifts, food, water, rest, horses' condition and encounters.
5. Offscreen NPC messages move only via available courier routes or established magic.
6. Update positions of party, wolves, wagon and separated NPCs independently.
7. Record arrival, spent supplies and local reputation; never teleport absent a demonstrated ability.

**Wagon:** Bracken/Morrow must be cared for; mountain passes may be impassable to the wagon, requiring a guarded camp. **Alpha:** may travel independently and need not follow a commanded route. **Dragons:** historic shrine locations do not guarantee a dragon currently resides there.
