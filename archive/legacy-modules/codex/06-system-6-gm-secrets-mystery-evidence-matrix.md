### 6.1a Approved sword-origin resolution and character knowledge boundary
**Decision SWORD-01 — USER APPROVED (out of world):** Both swords were forged by **one master smith**, using a **shared design** in **separate commissions**, with scales from **two different frost-line dragons**. The black blade uses the scales willingly gifted to Aric by the living black-and-silver Frostspire dragon; the white blade uses another frost-line dragon's scales. The World Bible's older GM-secret proposal is promoted to **[GM SECRET — LOCKED]** for these specific facts only. Neither donor's name nor the smith's name is fixed in the out-of-world reference by this decision. **Aric personally hired the shared master smith for his black-blade commission and knows that smith’s identity.** Eryndra remains only a candidate for the black donor, not confirmation. Vaeltharion is not thereby identified as either donor.

**In-world evidence boundary:** Theron currently knows Aric's black-blade commission, the white blade's recovery during Lyanna's investigation, and the matching runes/craftsmanship. He does **not** know there was one smith or two donors. Unlock those facts only through verifiable clues such as speaking to Aric (who knows the smith he hired), smith marks, commission ledgers, separate draconic material signatures, witness testimony or custody records. Do not make Aric mysteriously ignorant of his own hire.

**Chronology guardrail:** An ancient runic style does not date both physical blades. Keep Aric's ~26-year-old commission intact and do not invent the white blade's date. If earlier scene records are found that conflict, raise a reconciliation question rather than silently altering canon.

## 6.2 Villain constraints (no omniscience)
**Aldric Voss:** senior Crown-linked operator with national-security rationale, forged paperwork, limited agents, internal opposition. He can only react to evidence that reaches him and must spend time/resources.  
**Black Ledger:** compartmentalized; different operatives may disagree, defect, lie or protect their own interests.  
**Artificial Witness:** can imitate some authorization signatures but cannot create genuine Eighth Witness consent.  
**Fifth Bell:** depends on real pathways/credentials; not a spontaneous "plot timer".  
**Original Wardens:** do not collectively inherit blame for later successors' coercion.

## 6.3 Evidence provenance and clue fairness
Every mystery clue should be logged as: `clue ID | physical/source location | created by whom and when | truth supported | possible forgery | character who knows | alternative paths | date discovered | still available?`.
- No revelation from a single die roll without an evidentiary source.
- Every central mystery should have at least three viable clue paths; missed clues do not simply teleport into view.
- NPC lies must reflect knowledge/motives; contradictory testimonies are clues, not continuity mistakes.
- Player may read spoilers, but Theron cannot use them until discovered in-world.
- Before introducing a named dragon donor, sword smith, secret parent or culprit, compare all earlier canon and log the chosen identity and evidence paths.
