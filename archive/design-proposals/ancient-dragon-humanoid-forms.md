# Player-supplied ancient dragon humanoid references

Excerpts from the player’s design submission. Section numbers follow the submission. The source distinguishes proposed visual designs from a reported Aurelith scene.

## 3. Canon and approval rules

**Aurelith**

The earlier campaign conversation established that Aurelith appeared as an ancient silver dragon and subsequently transformed into a silver-haired elven woman with sapphire-blue eyes.

Treat this as player-supplied historical continuity requiring reconciliation with the repository.

Her exact measurements, clothing, apparent age, and other newly designed physical characteristics are player-proposed details, not independently authenticated historical facts.

**Other dragons**

The humanoid appearances below are player-proposed designs awaiting explicit canon approval:

- Vaeltharion
- Tharosyn
- Ilyssara
- Aethren
- Vharos

Do not automatically assume that these dragons possess an unlimited, free, or mechanically defined transformation ability.

Separate visual design from magical capability.

Do not introduce new spell costs, transformations, duration limits, combat advantages, or mechanical effects without appropriate approval.
